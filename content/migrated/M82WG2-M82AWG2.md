8" Two Way Active Installation Loudspeaker (vented) (Not Available in Europe) M-82(W)G2 Allround Installation Loudspeaker M-82(W)G2 which features the well-known and compact design of the M series is the powerful alternative to M-62(W)G2 speaker systems. The attractive design features a sturdy MDF housing and a very durable textured finish. The M-82(W)G2 is an excellent full range speaker with a deep bass fundament and a large frequency range which enable a very precise sound reproduction. White colour is available! M-82A(W)G2 Allround Sound Box It is also available as an active version with an integrated 80W Class A / B amplification module. Installation is ensured by the included SMB(Smart-Mounting-Bracket).
