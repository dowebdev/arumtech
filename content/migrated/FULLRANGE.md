12" Two Way Passive / Active Full Range Loudspeaker (Not Available in Europe) The V-12 is a two way full range loudspeaker with 500W RMS. The series consists of 8", 10", 12"and 15" multi-purpose speakers for an excellent price/value relation. The uniquely designed cabinets are all made of multiplex and painted with the Polyurea coating which helps stand up to the tough daily demands of touring and live applications.
