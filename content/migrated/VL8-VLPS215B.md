The V-L8 is dual 8" vertical line source concert system with variable curvature, primarily designed for live performances from small to large indoor and outdoor venuse. The power handling is 650W RMS each top. The V-LPS215B is a dual 15" , bandpass subwoofer with 1200W RMS. Offering plenty of tight and punchy bass, the V-LPS215B blends beautifully with the V-L8 or V-L4 and form an excellent full range response with high SPL and clear sound.
