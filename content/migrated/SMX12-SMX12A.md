STAGE MONITOR SMX-12A
The active SMX 12A is a compact, extremely versatile 2-way coaxial monitor in bass reflex design. Thanks to the built-in 12&Prime; LF with powerful 3&Prime; voice coil and a 1&Prime; HF with a 1.7&Prime; voice coil, the SMX 12A monitor is powerful and assertive in any mix and feature a harmonious, neutral sound character. This allows any musician to hear themselves better and bring out every nuance of their performance. The clearly audible signal in the 80&deg; x 80&deg; coverage angle is easy on any singer&rsquo;s voice &ndash; the more precise they hear themselves, the less effort is required for their performance. At the same time, the SMX 12A monitor remains uncomplicated and feedback-proof. The SMX 12A boasts a 2-channel Class-D amplifier with a total power of 484 W (LF: 416 W @ 6.5 &Omega;, HF: 68 W @ 14 &Omega;) on board. So there is no need for an external device &ndash; the internal amp is optimally matched to the speakers and brings eight DSP presets to make the set-up as short and simple as possible.
