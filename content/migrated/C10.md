Rigging System: 14&times; M8 rigging point, 1&times; 5 cargo tracks Cabinet Material / Coating: 15 mm selected plywood / Water borne texture coating Dimensions (W &times; H &times; D) : 332 &times; 542 &times; 344 mm Net Weight: 15.5 kg *35 mm pole socket DIAGRAM
Two way passive full range loudspeaker C-LINE engineering fixed-installations featured with high quality, wide dynamic range, high SPL and extended frequency response. It is reliable enough to be not only used for sound reinforcement of small-sized system, but also for zoned sound reinforcement of large-sized system. The uniquely designed cabinets are made of selected multiplex and coated with resistant water-soluble spot painting in black. Equipped with a complete set of fly ware, the series product can be perfectly applied to engineering fixed-installation. Additionally, the 90&deg;&times;60&deg;rotating horn realizes flexible switch in both horizontal and vertical direction, which helps meet the demands of multi-functional and sound reinforcement applications.
